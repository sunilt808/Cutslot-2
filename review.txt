### 🏛️ Relational Integrity (V4.1 Fix)
*   **Artisan Mapping**: The `Booking` model now uses a persistent `stylist_id` (ForeignKey) alongside a `stylist_name` fallback. This ensures that even if an artisan updates their profile, their ritual history remains historically accurate and linked.
*   **Indexing**: Critical performance indexes have been applied to `booking_time` and `status`, ensuring that the estate remains responsive under high booking volume.

### 🛡️ Security Protocol (Directorate Guard)
*   **Admin Isolation**: The `/reset-db` maintenance endpoint is now locked behind a **Directorate-Grade Access Control**. Only authenticated administrators can trigger a system regeneration, protecting the estate from catastrophic data loss.
*   **Idempotency & Race Protection**: The [Booking Terminal](file:///d:/Cutslot/frontend/src/pages/common/Booking.jsx) now implements a binary loading protocol and state suppression during ritual authorization, preventing double-charging and duplicate scheduling.

*   **Logistics Sync**: Real-time artisan transit statuses are now synchronized with guest-facing dossiers, ensuring a seamless luxury experience from atelier to doorstep.

### 📊 Directorate Oversight (Decision Intelligence)
